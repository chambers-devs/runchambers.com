Privacy Statement

Chambers by Runchambers has created this privacy statement to demonstrate our commitment to protect your privacy and to fully disclose what information we collect and how we use it.

At Chambers by Runchambers (hereinafter referred to as “Chambers”) we understand the need for privacy and respect your privacy rights.

Any personal information you provide to us including and similar to your name, address, telephone number and e-mail address will not be released, sold, or rented to any entities or individuals outside of our organisation except as noted below.

Data Collection

Chambers, the publisher of this website (including its subsidiaries and affiliates) will only collect and use personal data voluntarily provided by visitors to this website. However, visitors are able to browse the website without disclosing any personal information.

A visitor to the website may choose to provide limited personal data as required in order to register for certain services. Once registered, the information will only be used for the specific service(s).

Correspondence

If you correspond with us via email, the postal service, or other form of communication, we may retain such correspondence and the information contained therein and use such to respond to your inquiry and/or notify you of Chambers events, news or other Chambers-related opportunities. If you do not wish to receive such notifications, you may express your preference to not be so contacted by emailing us at hello@runchambers.com.

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to other websites of interest. However, once you have used these links to leave our site, you should note that we do not have any control over that other website. Therefore, we cannot be responsible for the protection and privacy of any information which you provide whilst visiting such sites and such sites are not governed by this privacy statement. You should exercise caution and look at the privacy statement applicable to the website in question.

Business Transfers

Your personal information may be transferred to another entity (either an affiliated entity or an unrelated third party) in connection with a merger, reorganisation, dissolution or similar corporate event. If such a transfer were ever to occur, the acquiring entity's use of your personal information will still be subject to this Privacy Policy.

Government and Legal Disclosures

We will not disclose your personal information which we have collected through this website, unless the law mandates us to do so.

Cookies

Cookies are pieces of data that a website transfers to a user's hard drive for record-keeping purposes.

This website uses cookies to provide enhanced functionality on the site (e.g., user ID and password prompts, and key resource access such as whitepapers) and aggregate traffic data (e.g., what pages are the most popular). These cookies may be delivered in a first-party or third-party context. Chambers may also use cookies in association with emails and other documents delivered/downloaded by/from Chambers. Our website also captures limited information (user-agent, HTTP referrer, last URL requested by the user, client-side and server-side clickstream) about visits to Chambers; we may use this information to analyse general traffic patterns and to perform routine system maintenance. You have many choices with regards to the management of cookies on your computer. All major browsers allow you to block or delete cookies from your system. To learn more about your ability to manage cookies, please consult the privacy features in your browser.

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. ("Google"). Google Analytics uses "cookies", which are text files placed on your computer, to help the website analyse how users use the website. The information generated by the cookie about your use of the website (including your IP address) will be transmitted to and stored on servers located outside of the European Union. Google will use this information for the purpose of evaluating your use of the website, compiling reports on website activity for website operators and providing other services relating to website activity and internet usage. Google may also transfer this information to third parties where required to do so by law, or where such third parties process the information on Google's behalf. Google will not associate your IP address with any other data held by Google. You may refuse the use of cookies by selecting the appropriate settings on your browser, however please note that if you do this you may not be able to use the full functionality of the website. By using the website, you consent to the processing of data about you by Google in the manner and for the purposes set out above.

URL and IP Address

Like many other websites, we collect information about our users' utilisation and navigation of Chambers. This information helps us to design the website to better suit our users' needs. We may also use your IP address to help diagnose problems with our server and to administer the website, analyse trends, track visitor movements, and gather broad demographic information that assists us in identifying visitor preferences.

Data Amendments and Retention

Information collected from visitors to the website will be kept in order to provide the required service and also contact information in relation to Chambers events and changes to the website. Should you require your information to be updated, amended or deleted from our records please contact hello@runchambers.com outlining your requirements.

Information on Children

As a business-to-business organisation, we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 18. Online registration and website usage is designed for adults who are professionally engaged in the businesses we serve.

International Transfers

Chambers complies with the European Data Protection Laws including the General Data Protection Regulation and in this regard Chambers ensures that any data transferred outside of the European Union which relates to data subject(s) based in the European Union meets the legal and regulatory requirements for international data subjects. Chambers takes the following approach when it comes to international data transfers:

  • Carrying out due diligence/risk assessment before international transfers take place that include personal data
