About Chambers

Chambers is an AI-powered legal software platform built around the way Indian advocates actually work — with active matters, complex documents, court dates, and teams that need to stay in sync.

The problem we saw

For many small and boutique Indian firms, the working day is spread across WhatsApp chats, email threads, Google Drive, phone storage, and physical files. Documents are hard to find. Tasks are assigned verbally. Deadlines depend on memory and follow-ups.

Chambers brings the matter back to the centre of the practice. It gives advocates and their teams one shared place to find what matters, understand what needs to happen next, and make room for the work that requires legal judgment.

One platform, three parts
01 — Documents

ChamberVault

A structured, searchable, and annotatable home for every case-related file and note. Share securely with your team, clients, and co-counsel.

02 — Work

ChamberFlow

A clear way to create, assign, track, and close the work around a matter — from court appearances to internal drafting tasks.

03 — Research

Judgment Portal

Search and read Indian case law without leaving the platform, then keep the judgments you find alongside the matter they belong to.

Who we build for

Whether they are working alone or leading a boutique firm, advocates need visibility without more administration. Juniors and clerical staff need clear priorities, useful context, and access to exactly the matters they are working on.

The people behind Chambers

We are a team of lawyers, software engineers, and product builders. We bring together first-hand knowledge of legal practice with the craft of building dependable software.

Chambers is being shaped alongside the firms who use it every day — grounded in the realities of Indian courts and designed to make modern legal practice feel a little more considered.
