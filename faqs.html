General

What is Chambers?

Chambers is a legal operating system built for Indian advocates and small-to-mid-size law firms. It consolidates case management, task workflows, document storage, legal research, and AI-powered tools into a single workspace so you can focus on your practice, not your software.

Who is Chambers for?

Chambers is built specifically for practising advocates who want reliable, fast case management on their phone; boutique law firms and chambers managing multiple cases and team members; junior associates and paralegals who need clear workflows and task visibility; and managing partners who want visibility and control without complex configuration.

Is Chambers available on mobile?

Yes. Chambers is designed to work seamlessly on mobile and desktop, reflecting how Indian advocates actually work.

Admin & Team Management

How do I invite team members to Chambers?

As an admin, go to Team Management and click “Invite Member.” Enter their email, set their role, and they'll receive an invite to join your workspace.

Can I change a team member's role?

Yes. Workspace owners can update roles from the Team Management section.

What if a team member leaves?

You can deactivate their account from Team Management. Their past work (Matters, Tasks, documents) remains in your workspace and can be reassigned to others.

How many team members can I add?

There's no limit on team members – add as per your firm’s team structure.

Support

How do I contact support?

Email us at team@runchambers.com with any questions or issues. Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 10 am to 5 pm IST.

What if my question isn't answered in the FAQs?

Check the how-to videos – they cover common workflows step-by-step. If you still don't find an answer, email us at team@runchambers.com and we'll help you.

Pricing & Billing

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. You can use Chambers free for 30 days with full access to all features. After 30 days, you need to upgrade to a paid plan to continue using Chambers.
